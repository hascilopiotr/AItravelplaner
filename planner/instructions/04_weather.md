# Weather

Each trip day may come with a forecast (or "no forecast yet" if it is too far ahead).

- **Rain, snow or storms likely (≥ 60%) or very cold (max below 0 °C):** make the day
  mostly indoor — museums, galleries, cafés, covered markets, malls. Keep outdoor
  stops short and close to indoor ones.
- **Mixed / some rain chance:** put outdoor stops (parks, viewpoints, walks) in one
  block and keep indoor options nearby.
- **Hot (max above 28 °C):** indoor or shaded stops around midday; parks and walks
  in the morning or evening.
- **Pleasant weather:** use it — parks, gardens, viewpoints and walking routes.
- If several days are forecast, put the most outdoor-heavy day on the best weather day.
- **No forecast yet:** plan a normal indoor/outdoor mix.
- Mention the weather briefly in the day's summary when it shaped the plan.

# How to structure a single day

- **Pacing:** aim for 4–7 stops per day. Fewer, well-chosen stops beat a rushed list.
  Leave the visitor time to actually enjoy each place.
- **Start:** begin the day close to the city centre or the day's chosen area, at the
  visitor's start time (not before).
- **Meals:**
  - Include lunch between 12:00 and 14:30 if the day covers that window.
  - Include dinner between 18:00 and 20:30 if the day covers that window.
  - A café is a good short break mid-morning or mid-afternoon, not a meal replacement.
- **Variety:** alternate types of stops — e.g. museum → park → café → gallery.
  Avoid two long museums (90 min+) back to back.
- **Indoor/outdoor mix:** balance indoor and outdoor stops; in the cold season or
  late in the day, prefer indoor stops for the later part of the day.
- **Durations:** use each place's `duration_min` as a guide; landmarks, monuments and
  memorials are short stops (15–30 min) that fit well between bigger ones.
- **End:** finish at or shortly before the visitor's end time, ideally with a
  relaxed stop (dinner, bar, viewpoint, café).
- **Routing:** order stops so the day flows in one direction through an area instead
  of zig-zagging across the city.
